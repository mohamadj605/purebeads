Dyneema-class engineering

The cord is the part you never see.

Most masbahas fail at the string. Silk frays, nylon stretches, and elastic perishes after a few months of skin oil. Ours are strung on braided UHMWPE, the fibre class sold as Dyneema® and Spectra®, doubled through every bead and tensioned by hand.

The knots are thermally bonded and sealed with a micro-thread burner.

Specification

Cord
Braided UHMWPE, 0.15 to 0.25 mm, chosen per bead bore.
Fibre
Up to 15 times stronger than steel, weight for weight.
Stringing
Doubled-over. Two strands through every bead, closed loop at the load point.
Tension
Calibrated so beads drape and slide freely, with no slack and no stiffness.
Terminal
Thermally bonded, then sealed with a micro-thread burner.
Warranty
Lifetime re-stringing, tied to the serial number on your card.

Dyneema and Spectra are registered trademarks of their owners. Cord strength varies by product and bead hole, so cord and bead are matched piece by piece.

Questions

Before you order.

How strong is the cord?
We use braided UHMWPE cord between 0.15 and 0.25 mm, depending on the bead hole, doubled through every bead. The fibre class is up to 15 times stronger than steel by weight. Ratings vary by cord and bead, so we match the two for each piece.
What does lifetime re-stringing cover?
If the cord ever fails or loosens, contact us with your serial number and we re-string the piece at no charge, for the life of the piece.
Are the stones natural?
Aqeeq and oud are natural materials, not glass or resin. The authenticity card records the material and lot for your piece, including any treatment.
How long does an order take?
Every piece is made to order in Toronto and dispatched in 3 to 5 business days. Shipping is calculated at checkout.
Which bead size should I choose?
8 mm sits light in the hand and travels well. 10 mm is the classic proportion, with a little more weight.
Can I add a name to my order?
Yes, and it is free. Choose Add a name on the product, then type the name at checkout. We laser-engrave it on a small wooden plaque, stain it and fit it to the box lid.
Do you take wholesale orders?
Yes, from ten pieces, each with a custom name plaque. and we reply within one business day.
