# Slide-top masbaha box: laser-cut files

Everything here is generated by `box_generator.py` (Python 3, no dependencies). `verify_box.py` (needs numpy) checks the geometry independently.

```bash
python3 box_generator.py --out .          # rebuild every SVG
python3 box_generator.py --kerf 0.18      # use a measured kerf
python3 box_generator.py --names names.txt  # plaque sheet from one name per line
python3 verify_box.py                     # geometry checks
```

## Files

| File | Send to the laser shop? | What it is |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `box_sheet_900x600_5sets.svg` | Yes, for production | 5 complete boxes nested on one 900 x 600 mm sheet |
| `box_single_set.svg` | Yes, for the first prototype | One complete box |
| `kerf_test.svg` | Yes, first | Five finger-joint coupon pairs to find the real kerf |
| `plaque_sheet.svg` | After converting text to outlines | 60 x 20 mm name plaques with sample names |
| `PREVIEW_box_single_set_labels.svg` | No | Same as the single set with part labels, for reading only |

Layers (units are millimetres, 1 SVG unit = 1 mm):

| Layer | Colour | Used for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cut | `#FF0000` | Part outlines, hairline 0.1 mm stroke |
| Score | `#0000FF` | Outline on the lid that marks where the plaque goes |
| Engrave | `#000000` | Filled shapes: lid border, ring and eight-point star |

## Dimensions (3 mm sheet)

| | mm |
| --- | --- |
| Outer box | 176 x 126 x 47 |
| Interior under the lid | 170 x 120 x 36 |
| Lid | 169.6 x 119.6 x 3 |
| Exit end wall height | 38 (lid underside is at 39) |
| Rails | 4 strips, 170 x 4 |
| Finger width | 8 (end fingers 11 to 12) |
| Parts per box | 10 (5 panels, lid, 4 rails) |

The interior is sized on paper for a 33-bead 10 mm masbaha coiled once, or a 99-bead 8 mm set coiled in three layers, with the tassel beside it. Nobody has put a real masbaha in it yet.

## Assembly

1. Glue the front, back and closed end wall to the bottom plate, then the exit end wall. The fingers press together. Check square before the glue sets.
2. The exit end wall is the low one. It goes on the end where the lid will slide out.
3. Glue the two **lower rails** to the inside faces of the front and back walls. Their top edge sits 39 mm above the outside of the bottom plate, level with the top of the exit wall plus 1 mm. Use a spare lid as a spacer: stand the lid on the rail and keep it level.
4. Slide the lid in on the lower rails, lay a 0.3 mm shim on it (two sheets of printer paper), and glue the two **upper rails** above it. Remove the lid before the glue sets so it cannot bond.
5. Stain and finish before assembly if you can, and keep finish out of the joints and rail faces.
6. Stain and glue the name plaque on the lid inside the blue outline. The outline is 0.4 mm bigger than the plaque so the plaque covers it.

## Ask the laser shop before ordering

1. **Kerf.** Cut `kerf_test.svg` in the exact material. The pair that presses together snug by hand, without force or wobble, gives the kerf. Re-run with `--kerf`. The default 0.15 mm is a typical CO2 value, not a measured one.
2. **Real sheet thickness.** Baltic birch nominally 3 mm is often 2.9 or 3.0, and walnut ply varies. Measure with calipers and pass `--thickness`.
3. **File handling.** Do they accept SVG with these colours and hairline strokes, or do they want a different stroke width, DXF or PDF? Do they set power by colour?
4. **Engraving.** Confirm they raster the black fills on the lid. Say whether they should engrave the plaques or you will.
5. **Nesting and minimum order.** They may want to re-nest. Five sets fit one sheet, so a 10-box wholesale order is two sheets.
6. **Text.** `plaque_sheet.svg` uses live text. Convert it to outlines in Inkscape or Illustrator (Object to Path) before sending, or fonts will be substituted.

## What has and has not been checked

- Checked by `verify_box.py` at zero kerf, on a 0.25 mm 3D grid: no overlapping material, no holes, nothing outside the intended shell, lid and rail clearances, and that every set fits the bed. All pass.
- Checked: the kerf rule (fingers wider, notches narrower, by the kerf value).
- **Not checked:** a physical prototype. Cut one box from `box_single_set.svg` and one kerf test before you order 50. Tolerances on the lid slide (0.2 mm each side, 0.3 mm above) are the most likely thing to need a tweak.
