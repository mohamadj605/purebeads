## (a) Executive summary: 10 most decision-relevant findings

1. **Ramadan 2027 is expected to start Monday 8 Feb 2027, Eid al-Fitr Tuesday 9 Mar 2027** (possible one-day shift; Canadian moon sighting decides). That is about 12 weeks from today. Confidence: Medium-High. ([Wego](https://blog.wego.com/ramadan-2027/), [Toronto timetable](https://www.halaltrip.com/prayer-tools/prayer-times/canada/toronto/ramadan))
2. **The Toronto-area market is large.** Statistics Canada shows Muslims at 10.2% of the Toronto CMA in 2021 (up from 7.7% in 2011); the City of Toronto is 9.6%. Confidence: High on share; the absolute count (about 626,010) is unverified. ([StatCan](https://www12.statcan.gc.ca/census-recensement/2021/as-sa/fogs-spg/alternative.cfm?topic=10&lang=e&dguid=2021S0503535&objectId=2_1))
3. **The corporate-order window is now.** Supplier guidance says order 3-4 weeks before Ramadan for plain boxes and 6-8+ weeks for custom-branded items. Inference: B2B offers must go out before about mid-December 2026 to land before 8 Feb. Confidence: Low-Medium (vendor blogs, not Canadian data).
4. **The premium tier exists and is priced well above your range.** Basmala Beads (own site, not Etsy) shows sale prices of roughly USD $160 up to about USD $1,223 for 99-bead pieces, with a lifetime-warranty claim. Your $149-$249 CAD sits at the low end of the premium tier, so there is price headroom for the oud/silver piece. Confidence: Medium. ([Basmala](https://basmalabeads.com/collections/all))
5. **The Canadian-made competitor is cheaper and bracelet-led.** Modasty lists 33-bead tasbih bracelets at about $43-$87 CAD, handmade in Canada, with sterling or gold-fill accents. No Canadian seller I found offers a dedicated aqeeq 33-bead tasbih in sterling silver at your tier. Confidence: Medium (gap may reflect search limits). ([Modasty](https://modasty.com/products/labradorite-tasbih-bracelet))
6. **Marketplace agate 99-bead tasbih sells cheaply** (about $43-$117 CAD on Etsy Canada). Your 99-bead and 33-bead aqeeq must be differentiated on proof (treatment disclosure, string, warranty, packaging), not on the stone alone. Confidence: Medium.
7. **Dyed agate is the main authenticity trap.** Sources say most inexpensive "carnelian" and much intensely coloured agate is dyed; dyeing carries a trade-wide disclosure norm (AGTA code D). Disclosing treatment honestly (and sourcing undyed banded stone where possible) is both a compliance protection and a selling point. Confidence: Medium. ([Skyjems encyclopedia](https://skyjems.ca/pages/encyclopedia-dyed-agate))
8. **Oud has the same problem.** Fakes are common and price is a rough filter (a "genuine" mala at USD $45 vs a tasbih at USD $399 appear in the same results). Provide origin, grade, and close-up photos or a lab note, or do not use the word "genuine". Confidence: Low-Medium (seller-written sources).
9. **Silver marking has a real rule.** A sterling quality mark ("925", "sterling") must be accompanied by a trade-mark that has been applied for or registered in Canada, applied by the same method. Marking is optional, but if you stamp 925 you need the trade-mark. Confidence: High. ([Competition Bureau](https://competition-bureau.canada.ca/en/node/478))
10. **B2B channels are findable but not packaged.** I found no GTA directory of Muslim realtors or Hajj agencies, and no Toronto corporate Ramadan-gift vendor with published bulk prices. That is both a gap to exploit and a risk (no benchmark). Confidence: Medium.

## (b) Recommendations: prioritized 12-week plan

**1. Launch with one flagship plus one, not four SKUs (weeks 0-2). Confidence: Medium-Low (no sales data found).**
- Flagship: Aqeeq 33-bead at 10 mm, $169. Optionally keep 8 mm only as a "slim" variant if production allows; otherwise drop it.
- Second: Oud and sterling silver 99-bead, $249, positioned as the limited, high-ticket piece (cap the first batch, for example 10-15 units).
- Evidence: Basmala sells both 33 and 99 formats across a wide price range, and has aqeeq plus oud 33-bead pieces ([Basmala red aqeeq oud 33](https://basmalabeads.com/products/beloved-red-aqeeq-oud-misbaha-bracelet-33-beads)); marketplace agate is crowded at the low end. Inference: fewer SKUs lets a one-person shop make fewer, better, photographed, tested units before Ramadan.

**2. Send B2B offers in the next 2-4 weeks (by about early December).** Target realtors, Islamic schools and centres, advisors with Muslim clients, and ERG leads. Offer a "Ramadan 2027 order-by" date (suggest 15 Dec 2026 for custom plaques; 15 Jan for stock pieces). Evidence: lead-time guidance above; Inference on specific dates.

**3. Build the "proof" page and make it the homepage hero (weeks 1-3).** Disclosure of stone origin and any dye or heat treatment, string spec (doubled braided UHMWPE), a real stress or pull-test video, silver mark, lifetime re-stringing terms, QR card. Evidence: dyed-stone and fake-oud concerns above; Basmala's lifetime warranty only covers beads and string, so a clear, simple warranty is differentiating ([Basmala guarantee](https://basmalabeads.com/pages/guarantee)).

**4. Do compliance basics before first sale (week 1).** Trade-mark filing (needed for the 925 mark), small-supplier HST check, Stripe fee modelling, a shipping price table. See section 8.

**5. Short-form video: 3-5 posts per week, process and proof led (weeks 2-12).** Stringing, knot-testing, unboxing and engraving, "what dyed vs natural agate looks like". Evidence is generic (vendor blogs); see section 5. Confidence: Low-Medium.

**6. Seed 10-20 units with nano and micro creators and community figures (weeks 3-8).** Budget guide: nano $25-$100 per post, micro $150-$500 per Instagram post and $200-$800 per TikTok (US-leaning benchmarks). Prefer gifted-product plus affiliate code over fees at launch.

**7. Set up Google Business Profile as a service-area business and a few local landing pages (weeks 1-4).** See section 7. Do not use a PO box or virtual office.

**8. Decide the personalization policy.** Keep the free plaque only if it adds under about 5 minutes and low cost per unit; test it on B2B (corporate or realtor name plaques) where engraved gifts are the point. Evidence is thin: custom or engraved pieces appear at USD $36-$500 on Etsy but I found no sales-volume data. Confidence: Low.
